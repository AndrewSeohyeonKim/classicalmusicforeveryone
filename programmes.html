01 Learning

Getting to Know Classical Music

Classical music talks for adults and older people.

For
Adults and older people who like the sound of classical music and never knew where to start. No prior knowledge needed.
What happens
Talk, recordings and live playing, in three stages: getting closer, experiencing together and finding your own taste. Usually six to fourteen people.
Where and when
Dublin · free · on request
How to join
Email us to ask about a talk. Libraries, parishes and groups can book one.

02 Sharing

Outreach Concerts

Live music in care homes, hospitals, religious houses and community settings.

For
People who cannot easily get to a concert hall, and the places that care for them.
What happens
Thirty to sixty minutes of acoustic music in the day room, chapel or atrium. Nothing for the audience to pay.
Where and when
Dublin, Co. Meath, Co. Wicklow, Co. Westmeath, and further by arrangement
How to join
Tell us your room and a date. We bring the players, instruments and stands.
Invite a concert →
03 Learning

Free Recorder Ensemble course

A free course for adults who have never played, ending in a concert.

For
Adults who have never played an instrument. No music reading needed.
What happens
A small group in a circle: a first sound in week one, your own part in the ensemble, and a short concert for family and friends.
Where and when
Mulhuddart Community Centre, Dublin 15 · Wednesday evenings, 7:00–8:00pm · free
How to join
Email us and we will tell you how to join. We can lend you a recorder.
Ask about the course →
04 Sharing

Letters Ensemble

Our ensemble of Dublin-based musicians, formed in January 2024.

For
Amateur players of strings, winds and more.
What happens
Irish and Korean traditional music, sacred music and accessible arrangements, played in community settings.
Where and when
Dublin · since January 2024
How to join
New players are welcome. Email us with your instrument.

05 Learning

Concert Guide & Companion

Going to concerts together, in small groups.

For
People who would not go to a concert on their own, including people new to Ireland.
What happens
Groups of about five. We prepare beforehand, sit together during, and talk it over after.
Where and when
Dublin · concerts such as the National Symphony Orchestra, the RTÉ Concert Orchestra and the NCH International Series
How to join
Email us and we will tell you when there is an outing to join.
Ask about an outing →
From 2027

CMFE Artists

We plan paid, mentored performances for emerging musicians, in care homes, hospitals, libraries and community settings.

For
Student, emerging, amateur and Korean musicians, alongside professional players.
How it will work
Professional, student and emerging players will be paid, in line with the Arts Council’s Paying the Artist policy; amateur players will receive expenses. Student and emerging players will go with a mentor on their first visits.
Now
Expressions of interest are open. We plan to open the first call, with its selection criteria, in 2027.
