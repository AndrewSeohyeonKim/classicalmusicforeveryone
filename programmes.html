Programmes

Our programmes.

Three help people listen and play. Two take live music out to the places where people already are. Each has its own page: how it works, what is on now and what it has done.

The five programmes

  1. 01Getting closer · Together · My taste
    Learning

    Getting to Know Classical Music

    Talks with live music for people who like classical music and never knew where to start.

    When
    On request
    On request→
  2. 02Day room · Chapel · Atrium
    Sharing

    Outreach Concerts

    Live classical music in the rooms where people already are: care homes, hospitals, parishes and community centres.

    When
    By arrangement
    Taking invitations→
  3. 03First notes · Reading · Your own part
    Learning

    Community Recorder Ensemble Class

    A weekly class for adults who have never played: first notes, then your own part in an ensemble.

    When
    Wednesday evenings, 7:00–8:00pm, autumn 2026
    Running now→
  4. 04Irish · Korean · Sacred
    Sharing

    Letters Ensemble

    Amateur musicians in Dublin playing Irish, Korean and sacred music in community settings since January 2024.

    When
    Ask us for rehearsal times
    New players welcome→
  5. 05Before · During · After
    Learning

    Concert Guide & Companion

    Going to concerts in small groups, with preparation before and a conversation after.

    When
    When there is an outing; we will email you
    Next outing by email→
