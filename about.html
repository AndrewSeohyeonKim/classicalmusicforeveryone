About us

Why we exist.

We bring live classical music to people who rarely get to hear it: older people, migrant communities and people in care settings around Dublin. We go to the rooms where they already are.

Our story

Since 2023.

It began in 2023, with a clarinet solo in Lourdes and a summer in the audience at the BBC Proms in London. In January 2024 Andrew Seohyeon Kim founded Classical Music for Everyone and the Letters Ensemble in Dublin; six people came to the first talk.

By August 2026 there were forty recorded talks and performances, in care homes, religious communities, parishes, a hospital, a homeless hostel and community centres. In 2026 a pilot recorder ensemble with seven retired Presentation Sisters led to a community class at Mulhuddart, and South Dublin County Council’s Arts Office selected our founder for South Dublin Live 2026.

The founder

Andrew Seohyeon Kim

Founder & Artistic Director

Andrew is a clarinettist, organist and community musician in Dublin. He holds a BMus (Hons) in Performance from TU Dublin Conservatoire and is Music Director at Our Lady of Dolours, Dolphin’s Barn. His final-year research was a ten-week recorder ensemble with seven retired Presentation Sisters at Warrenmount, Dublin 8. All seven completed and played in public.

How we are run

A not‑for‑profit initiative.

Classical Music for Everyone is a not-for-profit community music initiative, forming a company limited by guarantee. It is not yet a registered charity.

Who decides

Once the company is registered, an independent volunteer board will govern it. We are recruiting its first members now: a chair, a treasurer, a secretary, and leads for safeguarding and care, and for the community.

Money

We are not asking for or accepting gifts yet. No one acting for us will ask you to pay into a personal account.

Photographs and stories

We show performers, instruments and empty rooms. Anyone else who can be recognised appears only with their written consent. Our approach draws on the Dóchas Guide to Ethical Communications (2023).

The name

Why Everyone is in gold

In the mark, “for” is small and “Everyone” is large and gold. The last word of the name is the one we have to live up to.
