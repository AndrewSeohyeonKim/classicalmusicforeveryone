I.What we do

What we do: Five programmes.

Three help you listen and play; two take live music out to people.

  • 01Music history · Theory · Stories
    Learning

    Getting to Know Classical Music

    Music history · Theory · Stories

    Talks with live music, for anyone curious about Classical Music.

    On request
  • 02Day room · Chapel · Atrium
    Sharing

    Outreach Concerts

    Day room · Chapel · Atrium

    Live Classical Music in care homes, hospitals, parishes and community centres.

    Taking invitations
  • 03First notes · Reading music · Harmony
    Learning

    Community Recorder Ensemble Class

    First notes · Reading music · Harmony

    Running now

III.Why we exist

Many people in Ireland miss going to concerts, and still need the music.

  • Some live in a nursing home.
  • Some rarely leave the house.
  • Some are new to the country and don’t know where to begin.

So we go to them.
