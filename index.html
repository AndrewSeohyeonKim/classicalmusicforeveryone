I.What we do

What we do: Five programmes.

Three help you listen and play; two take live music out to people.

  • 01Getting closer · Together · My taste
    Learning

    Getting to Know Classical Music

    Talks with live music, for anyone curious about Classical Music.

    On request
  • 02Day room · Chapel · Atrium
    Sharing

    Outreach Concerts

    Live Classical Music in care homes, hospitals, parishes and community centres.

    Taking invitations
  • 03First notes · Reading · Your own part
    Learning

    Community Recorder Ensemble Class

    A weekly class for adults who have never played an instrument.

    Running now
  • 04Irish · Korean · Sacred
    Sharing

    Letters Ensemble

    Amateur musicians in Dublin playing Irish, Korean and sacred music.

    All players welcome
  • 05Before · During · After
    Learning

    Concert Guide & Companion

    Small groups go to concerts together, prepared beforehand.

    Dates by email

Musicians and artists can register interest any time. Volunteers can join the founding board.

III.Why we exist

Many people in Ireland miss going to concerts, and still need the music.

  • Some live in a nursing home.
  • Some rarely leave the house.
  • Some are new to the country and don’t know where to begin.

So we go to them.
