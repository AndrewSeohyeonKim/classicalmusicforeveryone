Community music · Dublin, Ireland

Classical music,for Everyone.

Bringing classical music where it’s needed!

Classical Music for Everyone brings live classical music, talks and a community recorder ensemble to older people, migrant communities and care settings in Dublin. We explain the music as we go, and we invite people to play as well as listen.

IWhat we do

Five programmes.

Three help people listen and play. Two take live music out to the places where people already are.

  • 01Getting closer · Together · My taste
    Learning

    Getting to Know Classical Music

    Talks with live music for people who like classical music and never knew where to start.

    On request
  • 02Day room · Chapel · Atrium
    Sharing

    Outreach Concerts

    Live classical music in the rooms where people already are: care homes, hospitals, parishes and community centres.

    Taking invitations
  • 03First notes · Reading · Your own part
    Learning

    Community Recorder Ensemble Class

    A weekly class for adults who have never played: first notes, then your own part in an ensemble.

    Running now
  • 04Irish · Korean · Sacred
    Sharing

    Letters Ensemble

    Amateur musicians in Dublin playing Irish, Korean and sacred music in community settings since January 2024.

    New players welcome
  • 05Before · During · After
    Learning

    Concert Guide & Companion

    Going to concerts in small groups, with preparation before and a conversation after.

    Next outing by email

From 2027 we plan paid, mentored performances for emerging musicians, as CMFE Artists. Expressions of interest are open now.

IIWhy we exist

Many people in Ireland never get to a concert.

  • Some live in a nursing home.
  • Some rarely leave the house.
  • Some are new to the country and don’t know where to begin.

So we go to them.
