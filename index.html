Why we exist

Many people who would love classical music are quietly left out.

우리가 존재하는 이유 — 관심이 없어서가 아니라, 장벽 때문에 음악에서 멀어진 사람들에게.

Not because they lack interest — but because of barriers: mobility limitations, health conditions, financial constraints, lack of information, or simply the feeling that “classical music isn’t for people like me.”

Classical Music for Everyone began in early 2024 as a personal initiative and has grown into a living, relationship-based model of community music. Rather than asking people to adapt to the traditional concert model, we adapt the musical experience to people’s real lives — and bring it to where they already are.

We are not a professional performance company. We are a participatory community where performance is a means of connection, not an end in itself. Music here is not a finished product delivered to an audience, but a shared experience — shaped through presence, participation, and time spent together.

우리는 전문 연주단체가 아니라, 연주가 ‘연결의 수단’이 되는 참여형 공동체입니다. 음악은 완성된 상품이 아니라, 함께한 시간 속에서 빚어지는 경험입니다.

How it works

Two pillars, one continuous cycle.

두 개의 기둥, 하나의 순환 — 배움이 경험이 되고, 경험이 나눔이 되며, 나눔이 다시 참여를 깊게 합니다.

Pillar I

Learning

Opening the door to classical music — gently, with no prior knowledge required.

  • Getting to Know Classical Music — a free monthly lecture series, in English & Korean.
  • Concert Guide & Companion — we attend live concerts together: before, during, and after.
Pillar II

Sharing

Turning experience into music made with — and for — the community.

  • Letters Ensemble — an open, participatory ensemble of amateur musicians.
  • Outreach Concerts — live music brought to care homes, hospices & parishes.
  • Older Adults Ensemble — a recorder ensemble for residents aged 78–92.

“Learning leads to experience; experience encourages sharing; sharing deepens participation.”

How we work

Bringing music to people takes wheels.

사람에게 음악을 가져가려면 ‘이동’이 필요합니다 — 우리 운영의 핵심.

For a “going-to-the-people” model, transport is everything — and historically our single biggest cost, around half of every outreach concert.

Carrying instruments, music stands and volunteers to nursing homes and rural parishes meant renting cars again and again. In 2026, thanks to community supporters, that changed.

We move performers, instruments and equipment to every venue ourselves, set up on-site, and stay afterwards to talk with residents. Every concert follows the same care: venue confirmation → programme → logistics → on-site rehearsal → performance → conversation.

Secured in 2026

Our community vehicle

🚐

A 2012 7-seater Citroën C4 Grand Picasso — room for the ensemble, instruments and equipment to reach communities across Ireland.

Funded by ~€4,000 in community donations + the founder’s personal investment. Ownership replaces repeated car rental, saving an estimated €4,400 a year.
