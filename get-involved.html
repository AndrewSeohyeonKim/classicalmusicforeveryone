01Partner venues

Partner venues: Bring a concert to your place.

Care homes, hospitals, parishes and community centres can invite us.

A concert

You provide

  • A room
  • A date
  • A contact person

We bring

  • The musicians
  • Instruments and stands
  • Music chosen for your listeners

A recorder class

You provide

  • A warm room each week
  • A contact person
  • Help letting local people know

We bring

  • Recorders to lend
  • Music and teaching, as funding allows
