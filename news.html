Timeline

Timeline: How it grew.

  1. 2024
    1. January

      It starts

      Founded in Dublin, with the Letters Ensemble. Six people came to the first talk.

    2. Through the year

      Music goes out

      Concerts in Co. Meath, Co. Wicklow, London, Paris and Daegu.

  2. 2025
    1. Through the year

      Into care settings

      Concerts at a day service, a homeless hostel and a care home.

    2. Autumn

      Introducing concerts

      We started introducing concerts to participants and organising group attendance.

    3. October

      Preparing the recorder pilot

      Preparation began for a recorder ensemble with seven retired Presentation Sisters.

    4. December

      At the National Concert Hall

      The fifteenth talk was a concert we heard together.

  3. 2026
    1. January to April

      All seven, to the end

      Ten weekly rehearsals at Warrenmount, then an Easter concert at Clondalkin Lodge.

    2. August

      South Dublin Live

      Our founder was selected. We played twice in Tallaght.

    3. September

      An autumn concert

      In Ranelagh, Dublin 6, at Methodist Centenary Church.

    4. Autumn

      The public class begins

      The class born from the pilot opens to the public in Mulhuddart.
