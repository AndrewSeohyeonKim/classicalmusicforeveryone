Learning

Community Recorder Ensemble Class

For adults who have never played an instrument. You play your first notes in week one.

Running now, Wednesday evenings, 7pm to 8pm, Mulhuddart Community Centre

Ask about the class How to take part
03First notes · Scores · Harmony
For
Adults who have never played an instrument
Where
Mulhuddart, Dublin 15
When
Wednesday evenings, 7pm to 8pm, autumn 2026
What you need
No instrument, and no need to read music

01How it works

How it works: From a first note to your own part.

  1. 1

    Your first notes

    Hold the recorder, breathe and play.

  2. 2

    Reading from scratch

    Notes and fingering, one at a time, with music we prepare for the class.

  3. 3

    Your own part

    The group plays in harmony. The term ends with a small concert of our own.

02What to expect

What to expect: Before your first class.

Do I need my own recorder?
No. We can lend you one.
Do I need to read music?
No. You learn to read a few notes at a time, as you play.
Where did the class come from?
From a ten-week pilot with seven retired Presentation Sisters, shown in the video below.
