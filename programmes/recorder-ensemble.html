Free Recorder Ensemble course

A term of weekly sessions for people who have never played anything, ending with a short concert for family and friends.

Join the Mulhuddart class → Other ways in

The friendliest possible doorway.

The recorder is gentle on the hands and breath, quick to a first satisfying sound, inexpensive, and made for playing together. That is why it works for people who have never played anything.

Nobody is asked to read music beforehand, and nobody is asked to practise alone at home to keep up. Scores and weekly handouts are written and printed by us, at no cost to participants.

Length
One term, weekly · 60–90 minutes a session
For
Complete beginners — no music reading assumed
Instrument
Descant recorder, €8–€15. Buy your own, or we supply at cost or lend you one
Ends with
A short concert for family and friends
Running now
Mulhuddart Community Centre, Dublin 15 · Wednesdays 7:00–8:00pm from 9 September 2026 · free

How it works

The first satisfying sound arrives in week one.

The shape of the term is fixed before anyone walks in. Reach something worth hearing early, move as a whole group so nobody is left behind, and finish in front of people.

Who it is for

  • Adults and older adults who have never played an instrument.
  • People who tried music once, decided they were not musical, and were wrong.
  • Anyone who would rather learn in a group than in a lesson.
  • People for whom a weekly reason to leave the house matters as much as the music.
  • New to Ireland, and looking for a room where the language is mostly notes.

What happens

  1. Everyone sits in a circle. Ten to twelve people, no rows, no stage.
  2. Warm-up on breath and one note, together, before anything is read.
  3. New material introduced by ear first, then on the page.
  4. Parts split so that every player has a line that is theirs.
  5. Something played end to end, every week, however short.

What we do not claim

The pilot is documented as Bachelor of Music research — a teaching plan, a dissertation and a concert programme. It is not a wellbeing study. Nobody’s health was measured before or after, and we do not present the seven completions as a clinical result. From the autumn 2026 cohort we are introducing a simple pre and post measure with a consent framework.
