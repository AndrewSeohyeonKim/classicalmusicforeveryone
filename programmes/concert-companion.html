01How it works

Before, during and after the concert.

  1. 1

    Prepare beforehand

    We choose a concert in Dublin and prepare together: what the music is, and what happens in the hall.

  2. 2

    Go as a group

    A group of about five goes together, with guidance during the concert.

  3. 3

    Talk it over

    Afterwards we talk about what we heard. There is no wrong answer.

02What to expect

Before your first outing.

Which concerts have you suggested?
The NCH International Series, the TU Dublin Philharmonic, the RTÉ Concert Orchestra, the RIAM piano series and the National Symphony Orchestra.
How big is a group?
About five. In December 2025 a group of six went together to a concert at the National Concert Hall.
New to Ireland. Is this for me?
Yes. The programme is for anyone who would not go alone, including people new to Ireland. We work in English and Korean.
