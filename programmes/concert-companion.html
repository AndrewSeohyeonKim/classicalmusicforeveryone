01How it works

How it works: Before, during and after the concert.

  1. 1

    Prepare beforehand

    We choose a concert. Then we go through the music and what happens in the hall.

  2. 2

    Go as a group

    The group goes together, with a guide during the concert.

  3. 3

    Talk it over

    Afterwards we talk about what we heard. There is no wrong answer.

02What to expect

What to expect: Before your first outing.

Which concerts have you suggested?
The National Symphony Orchestra, the TU Dublin Philharmonic and a National Concert Hall series, among others.
How big is a group?
In December 2025, six people went together to the National Concert Hall.
New to Ireland. Is this for me?
Yes. People new to Ireland are welcome, and we work in English and Korean.
