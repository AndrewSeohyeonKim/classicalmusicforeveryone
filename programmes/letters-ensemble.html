Letters Ensemble

An amateur ensemble founded in Dublin in January 2024, rehearsing every Saturday afternoon and playing in community settings.

Play with us → Other ways in

For people who play, and had stopped having a reason to.

Amateur musicians living in Dublin, most of whom learned an instrument once and then found that adult life removed every occasion to use it.

Repertoire is chosen so that any room can meet the music halfway — Irish traditional, Korean traditional, sacred repertoire and accessible arrangements. The ensemble is the group that plays most of the outreach concerts, which is why the programme is built the way it is.

How it works

Four concerts, and the hundred afternoons under them.

The concerts are the part that got written down. The programme is the weekly rehearsal, and for two years we did not record a single one of those. We are counting them from now on.

Who it is for

  • Adults who played once and would like a reason to again.
  • Strings and winds particularly, but ask whatever you play.
  • Players who want to perform without auditioning for anything.
  • People who would rather play in a day room than in a concert hall.
  • No grade requirement, and no audition.

What happens

  1. Saturday afternoon, about two hours, in a rehearsal room or a parish hall.
  2. Sectional work where it is needed, and full ensemble for most of it.
  3. Repertoire chosen with the next room in mind, not the next competition.
  4. Arrangements written in-house so every part is playable by whoever turned up.
  5. Concerts roughly quarterly, mostly in community settings.

What we do not claim

Over a hundred rehearsals happened and none of them were logged, so the ensemble’s record understates it badly. Membership numbers have varied and we have not kept a roll, so we do not quote a player count.
