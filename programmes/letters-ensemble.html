01How it works

Rehearse in Dublin, then play for communities.

  1. 1

    Tell us your instrument

    Write with what you play. Players of any background are welcome.

  2. 2

    Rehearse together

    We rehearse in Dublin, directed by our founder, Andrew Seohyeon Kim.

  3. 3

    Play for a community

    Concerts go to religious communities, parishes and care settings, often around a feast day.

02What to expect

Before you join.

What does the ensemble play?
Pieces such as Mozart’s Ave verum corpus, Down by the Sally Gardens and Arirang.
I stopped playing years ago. Can I join?
Yes. One member joined after a talk reminded them of the viola they played at school.
How many concerts has it given?
Four formal concerts, from Dalgan Park in March 2024 to Christmas 2025.
