  1. Who is responsible

    Classical Music for Everyone, a not-for-profit community music initiative in Dublin. For anything about your details, write to sby05034@gmail.com.

  2. When you email us

    • We use what you send only to reply and to do what you asked.
    • We never sell it, and only the services listed below handle it for us.
    • We keep artists’ details for up to two years, to offer them concerts.
    • Telling us about access needs is optional.

  3. Who else handles it

    GitHub
    Hosts this site and records IP addresses for security
    Google Fonts
    Supplies our typefaces and receives your IP address
    Google
    Runs our email
    YouTube
    If you play the class video, it receives your IP and may set cookies

    These services may handle data outside the European Economic Area, under safeguards approved by the EU.

  4. Your rights

    • You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you.
    • You can ask us to restrict how we use it, or object to it.
    • You can ask for a copy.
    • You can ask for any of these at any time.

    If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission.
